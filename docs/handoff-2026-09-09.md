## Assumptions pinned tonight

Each one has a fixture and a `docs/features.md` row; the comptable
questions page (`research/legal-fiscal/2026-09-08-questions-comptable.md`)
carries the ones that need an answer.

- Rates are basis points, at most 10 000; a rate above one whole is refused.
- Global discount spread: proportional to each rate group's HT, floored,
  the leftover centimes to the group with the largest HT, ties to the
  lower rate (`discount_spread_largest_remainder`).
- Stamp base is `total_ttc`, cash only, exact in centimes (the half-dinar
  question exists only if the tax must be whole dinars).
- Under IFU the TVA rows are empty and the unit price is the single price.
- Amount in words: French with 1990 hyphens including before `mille`
  (the JO examples space `mille`; comptable question), British English
  with "and", Arabic hand-written and unreviewed (`reviewed_by_native_speaker:
  false`; the 01/02 group pattern is the first thing to review).
- `amount_in_words` is computed on `net_to_pay` per the spec; décret 05-468
  says the TTC total. Open question for Samir and the comptable.
- A negative unit price is refused; an avoir is a positive-line document
  of its own kind, and a mixed exchange ticket is out of scope.
