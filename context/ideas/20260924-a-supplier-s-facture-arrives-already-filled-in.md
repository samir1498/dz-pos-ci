# A supplier's facture arrives already filled in

Samir, 2026-09-24, during the manual shop walk: the new purchase form asks
the shop to type "N° du bon fournisseur", the number printed on the
supplier's delivery note. If the supplier also used Dinar, could that
number, and the whole order, arrive by itself?

Today no: two Dinar installs do not talk to each other. A wholesaler can
already use Dinar (factures with RC/NIS, an optional wholesale price per
product), but nothing is built for them.

The idea: the wholesaler issues a facture or bon de livraison to a
customer who is also a Dinar shop; it is sent to that shop, and appears in
its Achats as a pending purchase with the lines, prices, TVA and the
supplier's number filled in. The shop checks what physically arrived and
confirms, which moves stock and the supplier's debt as a typed purchase does
now.

Needs: a way for two shops to exchange documents over the internet (the
same question as sync in `context/research/20260923-modules-sync-and-the-clinic-scope.md`),
matching the supplier's products to the shop's own (barcode first), and a
rule for a delivery that differs from the paper. Not planned; far future.
