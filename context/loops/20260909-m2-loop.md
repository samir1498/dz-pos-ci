# M2 loop

Same machinery as the M1 loop (`context/loops/20260909-m1-loop.md`): up to
four agents, the session manages, implementation agents in
`.claude/worktrees/<task>` on `m2/<task>` branches, red first, `just gates`
before every push and a push after every commit, e2e with a port pair per
task, reviewers read-only with the dz-review lenses, the session rebases
(or merges the milestone into a long branch when the rebase conflicts at
every commit), reruns gates, sends findings back to the same agent, merges
`--no-ff` with the `ctx:` trailer, and writes the ledger. Since 2026-09-09
18:21 the session also opens and merges the checkpoint PRs itself.

## Milestone branch

`m2/2026-09-09`, cut from the M1 head after the code sweep merged. Every
task branch starts from it and merges back into it; the checkpoint PR goes
from it to `main`.

## Waves (after the plan lens, 18:45)

- W1: T1 (migration: customers, debt ledger, allocations, and the
  `documents` rebuild with the customer FK, `quittance` admitted, the ref
  FK, the buyer block and the balance triple) alone.
- W2: T2 customers (routes and screen, adjustment endpoint) and T5
  facture_a4 template (builds its `Document` by hand).
- W3: T3 credit sale at the till (needs T2's customer routes).
- W4: T4 facture at the till (after T3; both touch `sales.rs` and
  `till.tsx`) and T7 payments and statement (after T2 and T3).
- W5: T6 avoir, cancellation and proforma (after T4, T5, T7; writes
  `debt.rs` after T7 does) and T9 avoir_a4 and proforma_a4 templates.
- W6: T8: the debt slip, the combined Pass 2 over T3, T4, T6, T7, the
  docs, the PR.

## Models

opus for the migration, the money and ledger services, the templates and
every proof review; sonnet for screens, i18n, docs and the boss page;
haiku only for counting.

## Ticks and stop conditions

As in M1: event ticks and a ten-minute heartbeat; never merge red; one
session owns the loop; two cargo builds at most on the box; a finding
that changes a fiscal rule stops the wave and writes the question for the
comptable in `docs/features.md`.

## Open before the goldens freeze

Open decision 4 (a real printed facture from an Algerian shop), R6 (native
review of the Arabic words file), R8 (the comptable: stamped receipt on a
later cash settlement, yearly series reset). None blocks writing the
code; the Arabic goldens are disclosed as unreviewed like M1's.
