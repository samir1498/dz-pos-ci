## Fiscal facts now read from primary sources

- Droit de timbre: Code du timbre 2026 art. 100-I and DGI circular
  14/2025. Per 100 DA tranche rounded up; 1 / 1,5 / 2 DA by band on the
  whole amount (no progressivity); nothing at 300 DA or less; minimum
  5 DA; no cap; electronic payments exempt (art. 258 quinquies). Only
  open point: half-dinar on an odd tranche count at 1,5.
- TVA: CTCA art. 21 (19 %) and 23 (9 % by tariff line). Rate per product.
- Rounding on a facture: no legal rule; CIDTA art. 324 governs the tax
  return only. Ours stays: centimes, once per rate group, half away from
  zero.
- Facture and ticket: décret 05-468 art. 3 (mentions), 4 (stamp and
  signature), 5 (TTC includes discounts), 10 (uninterrupted chronological
  numbering, a cancelled facture keeps its number marked "facture
  annulée"). Loi 04-02 art. 10, 12, 33, 34.
- IFU: CIDTA art. 282 ter (8 M DA threshold), CTCA art. 64 (an IFU shop
  must not print TVA). The shop gets a dated `régime fiscal` setting,
  `ifu` or `réel`; both must work in v1.
- Amount in words: décret 05-468 requires figures and words; French
  style follows the Journal Officiel (1990 hyphenation) and the Académie
  agreement rules; Arabic golden file hand-written from the JO Arabic
  edition and reviewed by a native speaker. Write our own converters in
  Rust; `n2words` only as a test oracle.
