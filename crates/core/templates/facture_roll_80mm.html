{#- The facture on an 80 mm roll. Standalone like its siblings, no external stylesheet and no image, so the bytes this renders are the bytes a browser prints and the bytes a server hands over (features.md §4). This is the layout that cannot say what the other three say. The A4 page, the compact A4 and the half sheet are one body under three stylesheets: same markup, same order, different type. A roll is 72 mm of content, so the two party blocks cannot sit side by side, a six column table does not fit, and a line becomes two rows rather than one. The markup here is therefore its own, and the test that holds it is the field by field comparison in `print_facture_roll.rs` rather than a byte comparison of the body. What does not change is what is on it. Décret 05-468 art. 3 has no roll version: both parties with their identifiers, the number, the day, every line with its quantity and unit price, the totals, the TVA recap under the réel, the droit de timbre on a cash sale, and the total written out in words. A roll that won its width by dropping the buyer's NIF would be a ticket with a facture's title. Every `amount-*`, `rate-*` and `qty-*` class is spelled exactly as the other layouts spell it, because the golden harness reads the amounts back out of the file by those names and compares them against the stored document. No length in this stylesheet is written as a percentage, for the reason its siblings give: an IFU document must not name the tax in any form (CTCA 2026 art. 64) and its golden proves the absence by reading the file for "%". -#} {{ facture.title }} {{ facture.number }} {%- if facture.arabic_unreviewed %} {%- endif %}
